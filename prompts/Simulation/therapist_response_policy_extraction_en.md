# Therapist Response-Policy Extraction

Extract conditional response policies from the supplied therapist examples and evidence.

Each policy must state its observable trigger, intended therapeutic purpose, permissible response progression, contraindications, exceptions, and source evidence. Keep framework terminology distinct from observed dialogue. Do not prescribe, diagnose, promise outcomes, or invent a response rule without evidence.

Return JSON only with provenance and limitations. A policy may be omitted when support is insufficient.
